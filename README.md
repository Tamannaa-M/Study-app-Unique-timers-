# While it lasts

A cozy focus timer where you study alongside melting ice, burning candles, or a mystery flight. Sessions track real focus time separately from object progress, hidden timers, and save history and productivity widgets in the browser.

## Features

- Five distinct ice and candle tiers with animated visual progress
- Mystery flights with destination tickets, changing cabin views, ETA, takeoff and landing
- Optional hidden timer with a final-minute reveal
- Multiple study spaces (6) , ambience controls, notes, tasks, music embeds and history
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Local browser storage; no account or backend required

This does not just mean another pomodoro timer clock which is obvious but brings something new to the table 
the flight durations are random you may get 5 min or 12 hrs based on the your luck but mostly it would be a doable time for studying. 

The main feature of this system is this lets you cheat and calculates the real time of what you studied and keeps you motivated.
